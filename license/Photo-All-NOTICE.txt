Spektra Studio acknowledges Photo-All as a reference for a physical lightbox,
movable film pieces, display-only lamp brightness, tiling, placement sizing
and rotation, a loupe, and contact-sheet,
single-photo and film-roll review, keep/reject/unmark workflow, automatic
advance, sequential saving and bounded shared previews.

The Spektra Studio viewer is independently implemented and uses its existing
native renderer. Photo-All source files, Python service, RAW runtime, LUTs,
recognition models and Motion library are not bundled by this integration.
See PHOTO_ALL_REFERENCE.md in the repository and distribution for scope.

观片台工作流参考 YZH2006517 及 Photo-All 贡献者的开放实现，感谢其贡献。
新界面、选片存储和缓存调度独立集成，原片、显影模型、调色和导出保持原有管线。
没有将对方完整应用或资源作为运行时依赖引入。
